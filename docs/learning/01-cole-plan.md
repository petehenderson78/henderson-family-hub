## 10. The rules that keep this sane

- **Five stars a week is the floor.** Four school nights plus one weekend set, or three nights plus two sets in a heavy sports week. Four stars by Thursday means Friday is free. Nothing is ever carried over.
- **Thirty minutes a school night** (12 math, 12 reading, 6 brain), 7:15 to 7:45, fallback 8:00 to 8:30. Nothing starts after 8:18; nothing runs past 8:30; bed at 9.
- **Skip checks and level-ups happen on weekends.** School nights are learn, review, read, play.
- **The thermostat:** above 95% independent for two weeks, pretest the next two skills; below 70%, a different worked example from the adult and one prerequisite step.
- **The benchmark wins.** If a district score falls while the dashboard shows mastery, a paper probe and a coaching call, and the district number is the truth.
- **Rewards are for inputs only**, capped at $4 a week, never for scores or mastery dates. "You're so smart" and "Wrong" are not said; "Not yet" is.
- **Sunday, five minutes:** ring, accuracy, skills mastered, retention, hint rate, fun check (thumbs 1-5). Two weeks of thumbs at 2 or below changes the mode, not the dose.
